# Habit Tracker: Game & Friends Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-23

This policy explains how Habit Tracker: Game & Friends (shown as "Habits" on your Home
Screen, the "App"), made by Stanislau Malchanau ("we", "us"), handles your information.
Short version: **we do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your habits stay on
your device and in your own iCloud account.**

## 1. Data the App stores

The App stores the following on your device: your habits (name, icon, colour, type,
schedule, target and reminder times), your marks for each day, pauses and days off, your
village (which plot each habit's house stands on and how far it has grown) and your
settings. Your Apple Watch keeps a copy of the same data to show and mark your habits.

We do not run servers that receive this data, and we have no access to it. The exceptions
are the timing of a running Live Activity (section 5) and anonymous usage signals
(section 8).

## 2. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App syncs your data through Apple's CloudKit, in the
private database of your own iCloud account, so that your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch
show the same habits. This data is stored and protected by Apple under Apple's Privacy
Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We cannot see, read or export it. You can
turn sync off in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

## 3. Habits shared with friends

You can invite friends to a shared habit. A shared habit is kept in a shared zone of your
iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit sharing. The people you invite, and only they, can
see that habit's name, icon, schedule and marks, and the name each participant has in
iCloud. Your other habits are never shared. A participant can leave a shared habit at any
time, and its owner can remove a participant or stop sharing; their copies of it then stop
updating. We cannot see shared habits or who takes part in them.

## 4. Headphone motion and spatial sound

With AirPods or other supported headphones, the App can use the movement of your head to
keep the village's sounds in place as you turn. This motion data is processed on your
device in real time, is never stored and never leaves your device.

## 5. Notifications, Live Activity and widgets

Reminders, the morning summary and the reminder before a free trial ends are created on
your device. Widgets and Apple Watch complications read your habits on your device.

While a routine runs, the App can show a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the
Dynamic Island. So that it keeps moving while the App is asleep, the App sends our small
relay server the Live Activity's push token from Apple and its schedule: when each step
starts and ends, random identifiers of the habits in it and how many are done. No habit
name, text you entered, name, email, account or device identifier is sent. At each moment
of the schedule the server asks Apple's push service to update the Live Activity, and it
deletes everything as soon as the Live Activity ends. The server runs on Cloudflare
Workers; like any internet service, Cloudflare sees the IP address of the request while
delivering it, and we do not store it. If you turn Live Activities off for the App in iOS
Settings, nothing is sent.

## 6. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements are reported to Apple's Game Center
and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center identity.

## 7. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive
your payment details. Apple tells the App only whether your subscription is active.

## 8. Usage analytics and crash reports

To learn which features help and where the App gets in the way, the App sends anonymous
usage signals to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service of TelemetryDeck GmbH (Augsburg,
Germany) with servers in the EU. A signal names an action in the App: for example, that a
step of the first launch was finished, that the subscription screen was shown or a
subscription started, that the App asked for a rating, or that iCloud sync failed. It
comes with a timestamp rounded to the hour and general technical details: the App and iOS
versions, the device model, the language, region and time zone, and display and
accessibility settings such as dark mode or text size.

A signal never contains the names of your habits or any other text you enter, your name,
email address, Apple Account, friends or location. Your installation is identified only by
a random identifier that the App salts and hashes before sending, so the signals cannot be
traced back to you or followed across apps. TelemetryDeck stores no IP addresses and uses
no cookies. We look at these signals only as statistics to improve the App; we do not sell
them, share them with advertisers or use them to track you. The App contains no
advertising or tracking code and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Separately, if you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings > Privacy
& Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage
statistics and crash reports. They do not identify you.

## 9. What the App does not use

The App does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health
data. The village's day and night follow your phone's clock and time zone.

## 10. Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from
anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

## 11. Data retention and deletion

Data stays as long as you keep it. You can delete a habit and its marks in the App at any
time, or everything by deleting the App and, for synced data, in iOS Settings > [your name]
> iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Habits. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing
further for us to delete. The relay server keeps a Live Activity's schedule only until that
Live Activity ends. Usage signals are anonymous and cannot be matched to you, so there is
no personal record of them to look up or delete.

## 12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA in
California, APPI in Japan, PIPA in Korea or LGPD in Brazil), you may have the right to
access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its
processing. Since we do not hold your data, you can exercise these rights directly on your
device and in iCloud as described above. You can also contact us with any request or
question, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

## 13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes,
tell you in the App.
