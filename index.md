# Habit Tracker: Game & Friends

A habit tracker where every habit is a little house in a cosy village, and a hedgehog
lamplighter lights its lantern each time you keep it.

## Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to **7tam.official@gmail.com** and we will
answer.

- To manage or cancel Habits+, open iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or Settings >
  Subscription inside the App.
- To restore a subscription on a new device, open the subscription screen in the App and tap
  "Restore".
