# Habit Tracker: Game & Friends

**A habit tracker that is also a cozy game: every habit builds a house in a hedgehog's village. 3 habits are free forever, and you can share any of them with friends. Habits is for anyone who wants a simple daily tracker that stays fun and never shames a missed day. Mark a habit in one tap from the app, a widget, the lock screen or Apple Watch, and the hedgehog lights its lantern. It works offline, syncs through iCloud and runs on iPhone, iPhone Duo and Apple Watch. Habits+ is a weekly or monthly subscription for up to 12 habits. No ads, no sign-up.**

[Download Habits on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6824098857)

## How it works

1. Add a habit: yes or no, a count like 8 glasses of water, or a habit to quit, every day or on chosen days.
2. Mark it in one tap from the panel, a widget, the lock screen, a reminder or Apple Watch.
3. The hedgehog walks to its house and lights the lantern. The house grows with your streak, up to a golden weathervane on day 100.
4. Read honest statistics: streaks, your best day of the week and your monthly rhythm.

## Who it is for

People building routines such as water, exercise, reading or sleep, or quitting a habit, who want a gentle tracker rather than a strict one. Friends and couples who want to keep a habit together. Anyone who likes cozy games and enjoys watching their progress grow into a village.

## Questions and answers

**Does Habits work without internet?** Yes. Your habits live on your iPhone and work offline. iCloud syncs your devices and shared habits when you are online.

**Is Habits free?** Yes. 3 habits of every type, reminders, widgets, Apple Watch and full statistics are free forever. Habits+ (weekly or monthly) unlocks up to 12 habits, the 12-month rhythm with trends and every holiday of the world. There are no ads.

**Can I keep a habit with friends?** Yes. Share a habit with up to 4 friends through iCloud. Their house stands next to yours, and you see their marks and streak, with no chat or feed. Joining a friend's habit is always free.

**What if I miss a day?** Nothing bad, and no shame: a neglected house can be repaired, and your streak picks up again.

**Do I need to sign up?** No. Habits uses your Apple Account and iCloud, so there is no new account to create.

**Which devices does Habits work on?** iPhone with iOS 26 or later, including iPhone Duo folded or unfolded, and Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later. It also has home and lock screen widgets, Live Activities, Control Center and Siri.

## Support

Questions, a problem or an idea? Write to **7tam.official@gmail.com**. We answer every message, usually within two days.

- To manage or cancel Habits+, open iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or Settings > Subscription in the app.
- To restore Habits+ on a new device, open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore.
- New iPhone? Sign in with the same Apple Account, and iCloud brings your habits back.
