# Habit Tracker: Game & Friends

The app page, its FAQ and `llms.txt`, and the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use of the iOS app
Habit Tracker: Game & Friends, in 14 languages, served by GitHub Pages. The legal pages arrive at
stage 10 of the app pipeline; `index.md`, `<lang>/index.md` and `llms.txt` are built from the
app's `docs/habits/site/content.json` by `docs/habits/site/build.py` in the factory repository
and copied here.
